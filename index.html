About us

About Matex

Matex is a professional manufacturer handling planetary gear module systems, gear increasing and reducing systems, transmissions, micro-processed parts, precision die production and precision injection molding. Founded in 1921, the Matex Group runs wholly-owned mass-production factories in Laguna, Philippines and Wuxi, China. STAR MATEX PTE. LTD. in Singapore is its administrative headquarters.

Quality Policy

Take initiative and realize customer's satisfaction. Self-satisfied QCD settings do not mean professional work. We can realize a business when we anticipate customer's needs.

Act based on the principle of Triple Spot Realism, run to the actual spot, look the actual articles and accept the fact, in all process and for all kinds of work.

Turn the cycle of PDCA speedily on all occasions and improve the efficiency of QMS systematically.

Analyze the reason of failure thoroughly and make rules as well as system learned from the failure, to prevent the same problem again.

Customer's claims and opinions lead to new products and services. Listen carefully and consider positively.
